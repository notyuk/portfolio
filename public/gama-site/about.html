About Us

GAMA Studio is a boutique residential interior practice, specialising in tailored interiors, bespoke joinery, procurement and full fit-out coordination across London, Turkey, Cyprus and the UAE. Whilst every project is unique, our philosophy remains unwavering: we believe every home should reflect the people who live in it, crafting spaces that are both beautiful and faultlessly functional.

TURKEY Bespoke production, manufacturing & supplier network
CYPRUS Bespoke production and regional residential project experience
UAE Residential interiors, procurement and full fit-out coordination
