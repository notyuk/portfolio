About Us

GAMA Studio is a boutique residential interior studio specialising in tailored interiors, bespoke joinery and full fit-out coordination.

Backed by decades of experience across London, Turkey, Cyprus and the UAE, we bring together international project exposure, craftsmanship and design-led execution to create refined residential environments. Our work is shaped by a balance of thoughtful design, technical expertise and attention to detail, delivering homes that are both beautiful and functional.

Drawing on high-end residential project experience in London, dedicated production capabilities across Turkey and Cyprus, and a growing presence in the UAE residential market, we offer an integrated approach to residential interiors — from concept development and bespoke manufacturing to procurement, project management and installation.

At GAMA Studio, we believe every home should reflect the people who live in it. Through careful material selection, bespoke detailing and a collaborative design process, we create timeless spaces tailored to modern living.
